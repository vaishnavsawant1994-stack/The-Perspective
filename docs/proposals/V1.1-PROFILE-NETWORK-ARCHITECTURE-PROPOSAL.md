# The Perspective Profile Network — V1.1 architecture proposal

**Status:** Proposal only; no V1.1 implementation or new route is authorized by this document.

**Prepared:** 31 October 2026
**Product proposal:** Public company and professional profiles connected to independent editorial research, source evidence, corrections, and the existing CRM/publishing platform.

## 1. Product intent

Create useful, source-backed public profiles for companies and professionals. A profile can introduce a subject, connect them to relevant people and published coverage, and give readers a clear way to suggest corrections. The work may later support relevant business relationships, but a CRM lead must never be treated as publication consent or as an editorial reason to publish.

The first delivery should be a small, editor-curated set of company profiles. Automated bulk profile generation, paid positive coverage, purchasable rankings, and paid verification are outside the proposal.

## 2. Existing platform pieces to reuse

- Existing `Person` identity in the `iam` schema and tenant-scoped `CrmCompany`/`CrmContact` records in the `crm` schema. Prisma does not currently define a shared canonical public `Company` model. `CrmCompany` is private tenant CRM data and cannot itself become a public profile projection. Do not duplicate or expose it; define a separately approved identity/linking contract before implementation.
- Existing `/people/[slug]` editorial/person route and `/author/[slug]` byline route are route-level surfaces. Prisma has no `Author` model; current author projections are derived from published editorial content. Preserve their distinct editorial meanings and confirm the underlying route/data contracts before extending them.
- Existing CRM company/contact and lead records, kept private from public profile projections.
- Existing editorial draft/version/review/publishing foundations in R8/R9.
- Existing authentication, organization context, authorization and audit foundations in R3–R5.

The public profile projection must be a separately reviewed editorial record. A CRM relationship or a verified identity match does not make the CRM record public.

## 4. Company Profile (PN-01) screen contract draft

### Public presentation

1. **Identity header:** verified canonical company name, logo/wordmark only where rights permit, industry, location/region, and a concise editorial summary.
2. **Overview:** sourced description of the business and its stated products/services, with clear dates and attribution for time-sensitive facts.
3. **Milestones:** a short editorial timeline; every factual item links to its evidence or published coverage.
4. **Leadership and relationships:** links only to separately publishable person profiles and relationships that have been reviewed.
5. **The Perspective coverage:** published articles, interviews, magazine features, podcasts, videos, and reports related to this company.
6. **Research notes:** a visible “About this profile” section that explains the editorial basis, “last reviewed” date, and how to request a correction.
7. **Commercial disclosure:** clear label and explanation for sponsored or commissioned material; commercial activity must not be presented as independent reporting.

The public page must not expose CRM notes, lead score, private contact details, outreach history, sales owner, client status, contract or invoice information, internal sources, or non-public research.

### Required states

- Loading, not found, unavailable, and stale/needs-review states.
- Profile with limited verified material; omit unsupported sections instead of filling space with generated claims.
- Correction request received, under editorial review, accepted, or declined, with an appropriate public correction note when needed.
- Subject relationship verified, clearly distinguished from editorial approval or endorsement.
- Sponsored/commissioned content visibly disclosed at the content and profile levels.
- Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile behavior with keyboard navigation and accessible source/correction controls.

## 5. Proposed publication and trust lifecycle

`Research candidate → source collection → editorial brief → fact check → editorial approval → versioned publication → correction/update review`

- Research candidates stay private and are not indexed, linked from public directories, or exposed through public APIs.
- Each publishable assertion must have an attributable source, captured date, and reviewer disposition. Uncertainty is preserved rather than converted into confident copy.
- A human editor decides whether a profile has independent editorial value. Adding a CRM lead or finding public data does not automatically create a profile.
- Publishing creates a versioned public snapshot. A subject claim lets an authorized representative verify a relationship and submit proposed changes; it does not grant editorial control or endorsement.
- Corrections enter an editorial queue. Acceptance, rejection, redaction, and removal decisions are recorded with an audit trail.
- If content is sponsored or commissioned, its commercial status is recorded before publication and shown to readers. Selling a positive conclusion, ranking, verification badge, or suppression of independent coverage is prohibited.
- Outbound prospect outreach remains a separate R6 action with its own lawful-basis, permission, approval, and delivery requirements.

## 6. Proposed data concepts — no schema changes yet

Reuse the canonical `Company` and `Person` records and connect them to separately versioned public editorial projections. Candidate concepts for a later approved database contract:

| Concept | Responsibility | Main boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ProfileResearchCase | Private research work queue and assignment | Internal organization scope only |
| SourceEvidence | URL/document citation, capture time, attribution, source quality and review disposition | Private by default; publish citations only when safe and rights permit |
| CompanyProfile | Stable public identity and publication state attached to canonical Company | Public projection excludes CRM fields |
| ProfileVersion | Immutable approved content snapshot and provenance references | Only approved versions are public |
| PublicEntityRelationship | Reviewed public company/person/article relationships | No automatic identity merge or relationship inference |
| ProfileClaimRequest | Expiring request and evidence that a claimant represents a subject | Claimant never receives editorial approval authority |
| ProfileCorrectionRequest | Proposed correction, privacy request, editorial decision and response state | Private request data; publish only the resolved correction where appropriate |
| SponsorshipDisclosure | Commercial relationship and reader-facing disclosure | Required before sponsored material can publish |

Names, fields, retention, constraints, APIs, permissions, migration strategy, and audit requirements must be frozen in separate V1.1 contracts before implementation. Similar names must never be auto-merged into one person or company.

## 7. Permission and privacy requirements

- Preserve the existing separation between Person, User Account, Membership, and Organization.
- Public reads return only the published profile projection and permitted source citations.
- Research, CRM linking, publication approval, claim review, and correction decisions require distinct server-side authorization.
- Use the existing R5 policy model; do not add permission keys or activate dormant permissions without a separately accepted authorization contract.
- Keep private contact data, CRM status, sales history, client contracts, internal notes, and unpublished research outside public output and search indexing.
- Review privacy/legal basis, image and content rights, retention, subject notices, correction/removal handling, and jurisdiction before collecting or publishing professional information.
- Identity claim verification confirms a relationship only; it is not a certificate of company quality, an endorsement, or an editorial conclusion.

## 8. Suggested release sequence

1. Complete the current V1 reconciliation and R14 gates. Do not add Profile Network code to the uncertified V1 candidate.
2. Freeze V1.1 route, screen, data, privacy, permission, editorial, and acceptance contracts.
3. Implement PN-01 Company Profile with manually curated, source-backed records and a private editorial workflow.
4. Add claim and correction workflows only after their identity, privacy, notification, and editorial contracts are accepted.
5. Add directories, research studio automation, profile analytics, and CRM outreach connections as separately measured follow-on work.

## 9. PN-01 acceptance criteria for a future implementation

- The page uses a canonical Company record and an approved immutable public profile version.
- Every factual statement has provenance or is clearly attributed as a company-supplied statement.
- No private CRM/contact/client information is returned by public route, metadata, sitemap, search, or related-entity APIs.
- Draft, review, approval, publication, correction, sponsorship, and claimant actions are server-authorized and auditable.
- A claim cannot alter editorial content or mark independent coverage as endorsed.
- Sponsored content is visibly disclosed and cannot purchase positive factual conclusions or rankings.
- Unknown slugs return the not-found boundary; unpublished profiles cannot be discovered publicly.
- Required desktop/mobile accessibility, keyboard, loading/error, source/correction, and hostile authorization cases pass exact-head qualification.

These criteria are a proposal for owner review. They do not authorize routes, migrations, permissions, provider integrations, public profile publication, or production changes.
