# V1 product surface (frozen for R14 planning)

STATUS: **SURFACE DESCRIPTION — NOT A CERTIFICATION**

This is the accepted R1–R13 product. R14 does not add a capability to it.

## Included

- Public editorial site, including home, newsroom, magazine catalogue, and the R9 reader for readable issues only.
- Team workspace under `/app`, including the R13 operations desk at `/app/operations/desk`.
- Client desk at `/client/desk`.
- Member library at `/my-perspective/library`.
- Authentication APIs under `/api/v1/auth`.
- Domain APIs under `/api/v1/r6` through `/api/v1/r13` as already accepted.
- Workers: R9 publish-due, R10 reconcile, R11 reindex/run, R12 grant/offers/revoke. Each requires its server token.
- Schemas: `audit`, `commercial`, `comms`, `crm`, `iam`, `platform`, `ops`.
- Permission registry: 131 frozen keys. Active keys are only those already activated through R13. Dormant keys stay dormant.

The route and permission inventories are generated by `scripts/certification/inventory-surface.mjs` at qualification time. A hand-copied list of all 200+ pages is not the source of truth.

## Operations the surface actually supports

- Sign-in, session, MFA, recovery, and invitation acceptance as accepted in R3.
- Tenant-scoped CRM, communications, and commercial commands as accepted in R6.
- Proposals, contracts, signatures, invoices, and payment reconciliation as accepted in R7, without a browser-asserted paid or signed flag.
- Editorial production commands as accepted in R8.
- Publishing and reader access as accepted in R9.
- Media distribution attempts that fail closed without a provider, as accepted in R10.
- Search, SEO, and automation as accepted in R11.
- Client projections and member entitlements as accepted in R12. Checkout returns `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`.
- Organization preference rows and integration declarations as accepted in R13. Verify returns `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`.

## Explicit exclusions

- No notification or email delivery.
- No object-storage upload product.
- No AI assistant.
- No incident-management API.
- No second subscription ledger.
- No live payment, signature, distribution, or integration provider.
- No production host, domain, or customer data.
- No Design 154 and no page 58+ as a new design.
- Draft pull requests #8 and #10.
- V1.0 certification and production-ready wording.

## Route classes

- Public: marketing and editorial pages, `/login`, `/api/contact`, and the R14 liveness/readiness routes.
- Authenticated team: `/app/**` and team APIs.
- Client: `/client/**` and client-scoped reads.
- Member: `/my-perspective/**`.
- Worker: `/api/v1/internal/**` with the matching server token.
- Provider callback: none. A callback must not be invented.

Anonymous requests to `/app` and `/client` redirect to sign-in. That remains the abuse boundary for those surfaces. Process-local rate limits are not added; they would not survive more than one instance and no edge control exists yet.
